What this server keeps

Shroud is built so the server can pass messages along without reading them, or saying who you are.

  • Messages
    On my way 17:53 0.2 KB 17:53

    A sealed envelope, delivered and then forgotten. It opens only on your devices.

  • Username
    MLmara 3f9a 1c0e b7d4 …

    A slow, salted hash. Nobody looks you up by name, the operator included.

  • Contacts
    MLMara Lindqvist

    Two accounts that may message each other. The names you give people travel sealed.

  • Devices
    iPhoneWork laptop

    Up to five slots, so you can revoke one. Their names are sealed too.

  • Calls
    12:04 rang, answered

    A ring and a handshake. Audio and video go between your devices, or through a relay that cannot listen.

  • Phone, email
    Phone number nothing

    Never asked for. There is nothing here to link to a person.
