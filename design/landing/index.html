How Shroud keeps it private

The browser is the same client as on your iPhone, with the same rules.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Messages decrypt only on your devices. Everything in between is ciphertext.

  • A first-class device

    This browser is one of up to five linked devices, with the same chats as your iPhone.

  • Safety numbers

    Compare keys in person before you trust a change. A new key is something you see, not something that just happens.

  • No phone number, no email

    A username and a 12‑word encryption phrase are the whole account. Write the phrase down; it is the only way back in.
